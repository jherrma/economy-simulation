# The order of the tick, as a committed fixture. Simulation.StepOrder is compared against it.

# Adding or reordering a step is a deliberate edit to this file, and needs a reason. Three of the
# orderings have a failure mode that produces a working run with wrong numbers:
#
#   debt service before the walk  — otherwise households spend their way into arrears, which v1
#                                   has no machinery to handle
#   ageing after the walk         — otherwise a unit bought this tick is immediately wanted again
#   repricing after the walk      — otherwise the household visited first faces a different price
#                                   from the one visited last, and rationing order becomes a price
#                                   advantage
#
# Restocking happens before step 1 and is not a step: nothing decides and nothing moves.
